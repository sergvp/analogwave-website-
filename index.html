Services

Engineering for the hard parts of hardware

Take one piece or the whole design. We fit into your team at any stage, from architecture to production test.

System architecture

Requirements, block diagrams, part selection and power budgets, settled before layout starts.

  • Component and supply-chain review
  • Battery life and throughput estimates
  • Design reviews of existing work

Analog & mixed-signal

Precision and low-noise circuits where every microvolt counts.

  • ECG / EXG biopotential front ends
  • Sensor interfaces, ADC and DAC chains
  • Input protection, filtering, grounding

Audio: vacuum tube to Class D

Amplifiers that sound right and measure right, from glowing tubes to switching power stages.

  • Vacuum tube amps and preamps, HV supplies, output transformers
  • Class A and AB discrete solid-state designs
  • Class D amplifiers: output filters, feedback, EMI
  • Low-noise preamps, phono stages, DAC and ADC paths
  • THD+N, noise and frequency response measurements

RF & microwave Core

Radios that meet their link budget and pass emissions on the first try.

  • BLE, Wi-Fi, sub-GHz and custom radios
  • Matching networks, antenna tuning, VNA work
  • Controlled-impedance stackups and transmission lines
  • Filters, LNAs, PAs and microwave layout

Medical electronics Core

Patient-connected devices designed with safety and regulation in mind from day one.

  • ECG, EEG, EMG and other biopotential recorders
  • Wearable and ambulatory monitors, low-power design
  • Patient isolation, defibrillation and ESD protection
  • Designed toward IEC 60601-1 / -1-2 and ISO 14971, with design documentation

PCB design & layout

Multi-layer and multi-board designs, built to be manufactured.

  • Altium schematic capture and layout
  • BGA, fine-pitch and board-to-board connectors
  • DFM, DFT and BOM pricing

Signal integrity optimization

Clean edges and open eyes on fast buses, found in simulation before they show up on the bench.

  • Impedance control, termination and length matching
  • Crosstalk, return paths and reference-plane gaps
  • Pre- and post-layout simulation, TDR and eye-diagram checks

Power integrity

Quiet, stable rails for sensitive analog, RF and fast digital circuits on the same board.

  • Power delivery network (PDN) impedance targets
  • Decoupling strategy, plane design and placement
  • Low-noise regulators and switcher ripple and EMI control
  • DC IR-drop analysis and rail noise measurements

Embedded firmware

Bare-metal and RTOS firmware in C and C++, written to save power.

  • STM32 and other ARM Cortex-M MCUs
  • USB, BLE, SPI, SDMMC, file systems
  • Low-power modes and bootloaders

Brushless motor (BLM) drivers

Efficient, quiet drives for brushless DC and PMSM motors, from power stage to control loop.

  • Three-phase MOSFET and GaN power stages, gate drivers
  • Sensorless and sensored control: FOC, six-step, Hall and encoder
  • Phase current sensing and fast fault protection
  • Low-EMI layout and thermal design

Prototype bring-up Core

We turn a fresh board into a working prototype.

  • First power-on, rails and clocks checked
  • Debugging with scope, spectrum and network analyzers
  • Rework, fixes and a clean next revision
  • Validation reports you can hand to your team

Test & compliance prep

Fewer surprises at the lab.

  • FCC, CE and IEC 60601 pre-scans
  • Manufacturing test fixtures
  • Battery and power characterization
Process

Small steps you can test, not one big release

You get hardware and firmware that work at every milestone, so problems show up early when they are cheap to fix.

Define

Requirements, constraints and a budget we agree on.

Design

Schematics, simulations, RF and layout with reviews along the way.

Build

Fab, assembly and parts sourcing handled with your vendors or ours.

Bring up

Power-on, debug and measure until the prototype meets spec.

Hand off

Files, test reports and support for the production run.
